FieldCred vs spreadsheet vs myComply

Three different jobs. Mixing them up is how a sub pays for a GC’s platform and still bounces at the gate — or skips the GC’s platform and never gets on the site.

Safety managers get sold “compliance software” as if it were one product. It is not. A spreadsheet is your file. FieldCred is your file with expirations and a public QR. myComply is often the GC’s project network. SkillSignal and Breadcrumb sit in that GC-network category too. FieldCred is not trying to be SkillSignal for ENR general contractors.

Spreadsheet

Fine for a 12-person shop that never gets audited at 6 a.m. It stores. It does not warn. It is not in the guard’s pocket. When the keeper is on PTO, the file is a rumor. If that is still your system, export a clean CSV once so you can see the holes — sample audit CSV.

FieldCred

Built for the contractor or staffing firm that owns the crew. Upload certs, track recency, alert before the date you care about, and hand the gate a QR that works without an account. OSHA 10/30 stays an admin lookup plus an attestation; there is no OSHA API. See how to verify an OSHA 10.

Pricing is per active credentialed worker, billed annually, not per scan or per QR view. As published on www.fieldcred.co/#pricing (August 2026): Essential $249/mo up to 75 workers; Professional $499/mo up to 250; Enterprise custom (500+). Launch offer: $299/mo up to 100. Request a demo there — do not treat a blog post as a quote.

myComply

myComply is widely used when a general contractor or construction manager runs their sites on it. GCs often mandate it. FieldCred does not replace that mandate. If the GC says enroll, you enroll — or you accept a sponsored seat if they buy the seats.

Published company-license pricing on mycomply.net/info/pricing as of 27 August 2026 (USD, annual):

  • 1–19 employee profiles: $499/year
  • 20–99 profiles: $999/year
  • 100+ profiles: $1,999/year
  • Pro Pack (extra modules / self-perform): $6,999/year and $8,999/year tiers on that page

GCs also run enrolled-sub (you buy your own company license) and sponsored-sub (GC buys seats) models. Smart Badge is in play when the GC uses it. Confirm current numbers on their site before you budget; we are not myComply and this is not their quote.

Do not pick one and pretend the other disappeared. A GC-mandated myComply login gets you onto that project. It does not keep your next dispatch current for a different GC, a shutdown, or a customer who only wants a CSV and a QR. Keep your own roster. Pay for the GC’s tool when they require it.

SkillSignal, Breadcrumb, and other GC stacks

Same rule. If an ENR GC’s gate runs on SkillSignal or Breadcrumb, that is their access control. FieldCred is not a bid to replace it. Use FieldCred so your people are already current — cards verified, dates real, QR ready — before they hit the GC’s system. For the bounce itself, use the staffing gate-rejection playbook.
