Fair questions

Asked and answered.

"My guys won't use an app."

They don't have to. Workers carry a QR code; supervisors scan it. Zero worker logins, zero training rollout for the crew.

"HR already stores the certs."

Storage isn't verification. HR answers "do we have a copy." FieldCred answers "is this worker cleared, right now, at this gate." Run it alongside HR, not instead of it.

"The spreadsheet works fine."

Until the person who runs it is on vacation, or the audit lands. The spreadsheet stores; it doesn't warn — and it isn't standing at the gate.
