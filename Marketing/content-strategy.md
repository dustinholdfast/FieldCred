# FieldCred — Content Strategy

**What this file is:** The operational plan — which channels, what content, how often, driving toward what. Pairs with brand-voice.md (how we sound), audience-icp.md (who we're talking to), and messaging-positioning.md (what we say). Shipped work gets logged in content-calendar.md.

**Stage note (confirmed 2026-07-18):** FieldCred is pre-launch; the funnel goal is pilot signups, and the primary CTA is **join the pilot — free early access** (Dustin's pick). No pricing content beyond "free during pilot"; no traction claims of any kind.

> Assumption: Channel picks and cadence are carried over from Dustin's Holdfast decisions (email + SEO engine, LinkedIn as repurposing layer, moderate cadence) as defaults — they were never chosen for FieldCred specifically. Confirm before running this plan at full cadence; where pilot users actually come from should reshape it.

---

## Channel strategy

**Engine — Website / SEO articles.** The core asset. Practical, plain-English guides answering the exact questions a safety manager or contractor types into Google after a gate incident or before an audit. Each article is written once and reused everywhere else.

**Nurture — Email newsletter.** Software purchases at this size have a real evaluation cycle; the newsletter keeps FieldCred in the room between the gate moment and the signup. Field-compliance notes, regulation and renewal reminders, one useful thing per issue. Grows from article readers and demo leads.

**Distribution — LinkedIn company page.** Publishes under the FieldCred brand voice. Role: repurpose and amplify, not originate — article excerpts, checklist fragments, newsletter highlights, 3–4 posts/week cut from the engine content.

**Not active (for now):** Personal-profile LinkedIn content, paid ads, in-person event support. Revisit quarterly.

---

## Content pillars

Each maps to a messaging pillar in messaging-positioning.md. Every piece of content belongs to exactly one.

**P1 — The gate moment** *(maps to "Cleared to work")*
For the prospect right after the system failed in public. What to do when a worker gets bounced, when the GC demands records, when mobilization is Monday.
*Article seeds:* "The GC asked for your crew's training records — here's what to send" · "A worker got turned away at the gate: the 48-hour playbook" · "What GCs actually check before letting your crew on site."

**P2 — Nothing lapses silently** *(maps to expiration tracking)*
The quiet failure mode. How certs actually lapse, renewal timelines by credential type, building a renewal calendar.
*Article seeds:* "Why certs expire quietly (and how to catch them 60 days out)" · "OSHA 10 vs. OSHA 30: who needs which, and when they expire" · "The renewal calendar every safety manager should run."

**P3 — Compliance in plain English** *(maps to "The field doesn't do logins" / the translator)*
Glossary-killers and process demystifiers. One concept per piece, jobsite English.
*Article seeds:* "What counts as a 'current' operator cert" · "What OSHA actually asks for after an incident" · "Does your HR system really track certs? How to check."

**P4 — Audit-ready is a report** *(maps to proof on demand)*
The business case. Audit prep, client questionnaires, insurance reviews, the cost of the scramble.
*Article seeds:* "Cert tracking isn't paperwork — it's whether your crew works Monday" · "What a training-records audit looks like when you're ready (and when you're not)."

## Cadence (moderate tier — carried over from Holdfast)

| Output | Frequency | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SEO article | 1/week | Original, from pillar seeds |
| Newsletter issue | Biweekly | Original + article recap |
| LinkedIn company-page posts | 3–4/week | Repurposed from articles/newsletter |

Review load on Dustin: roughly one article + one batch of posts per week, one newsletter every other week. If review becomes the bottleneck, drop to 2 posts/week before dropping the article — the article is the engine.

---

## Funnel and CTA

**Primary CTA everywhere: join the pilot — free early access → https://pilot.fieldcred.co.** Every article, newsletter issue, and post points to it. Honest about the stage, removes the cost objection entirely, and builds the pilot cohort whose feedback and origin channels shape everything else.

**Secondary CTA (articles only): newsletter signup.** For readers not ready to commit a roster. "Get the next one" is an easy yes.

Path: search/post → article → pilot signup (hot) or newsletter (warm) → newsletter nurtures until the next gate moment or audit makes them hot.

---

## Rules of engagement

1. Every piece complies with brand-voice.md. No fear-selling, no implied OSHA/issuer endorsement, no traction claims, no pricing beyond "free during pilot," jargon defined.
2. Every piece targets one ICP reader (owner/ops, safety manager, or staffing lead — per audience-icp.md) and one pillar.
3. Every piece carries the primary CTA. No orphan content.
4. Nothing publishes or sends without Dustin's explicit approval. Claude drafts; Dustin ships.
5. Shipped work gets logged in content-calendar.md in the same session.

## Metrics that matter

Pilot signups (the only number that matters right now) · **where each pilot signup came from** (this is the future channel strategy) · newsletter list growth · article organic traffic and which pillars drive it. Review monthly; kill what's not working after 90 days, not 9.
